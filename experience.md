### Kero-Sine

I'm building **Kero-Sine**, a SQL compiler on top of MLIR. It's open source and
still in development. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/PyDevC/kero).

### Neovim tooling

I made **model-cmp.nvim**, an AI autocomplete plugin for Neovim, and the
[model_server](https://github.com/PyDevC/model_server) it runs on. I still
maintain both.
